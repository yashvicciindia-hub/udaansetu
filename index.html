The platform

What is UdaanSetu?

UdaanSetu brings people, opportunities, institutions, services and markets together into a connected livelihood ecosystem.

It helps individuals and enterprises find the relevant support to start, strengthen and grow their livelihood or business.

The problem

Today, one journey means many disconnected doors.

Starting or growing a livelihood means separately navigating training providers, banks, government offices, suppliers, and buyers — repeating the same story at every step.
