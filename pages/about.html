Why we exist

India's livelihood ecosystem is broken. We're building the bridge.

"Udaan" means flight, and "Setu" means bridge — UdaanSetu is the bridge that lets a livelihood journey take flight.

The problem

People are forced to piece the journey together on their own.

Separate forms and approvals at government offices. Skills training with no clear path to income. Credit that's hard to access in practice. Strong but project-based NGO support. Slow, complex registration and compliance. Hard-to-find reliable suppliers. Weak packaging that limits market readiness. Informal market access with low bargaining power. Manual logistics that slow everything down. And once a product finally reaches the market — no sustained support to keep it there.

India lacks livelihood integration. The result is delay, duplication and weak follow-through — for people who often have the least room to absorb it.

Our vision

One platform. Every connection.

We connect government programmes, finance, skilling, logistics and market access in one layer. People move through a single journey — onboarding, training, services, financing, production and sales — instead of starting over at every stage. Partners coordinate from one shared view, with less friction and faster follow-through.

The ecosystem approach

We don't replace what exists. We connect it.

Government, banks, training institutes, NGOs, logistics providers and buyers already do valuable work. UdaanSetu doesn't compete with any of them — it's the connective layer that lets a person move between them without repeating their story or losing momentum.

Where we're headed

Growing ecosystem by ecosystem, not city by city.

We start by proving the model in one district, then scale state by state through aligned partners — building toward a national backbone for India's livelihood economy. See our full impact and vision roadmap.
