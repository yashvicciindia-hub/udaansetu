How UdaanSetu works.

From first contact to steady earnings, the platform guides each person through a clear, supported pathway — while ecosystem partners stay coordinated behind the scenes.

01

Join & Assess

Create a unified profile that captures your goals, skills, location and needs. UdaanSetu matches you to the right programmes and partners from day one — no repeating your story at every office.

02

Discover & Skill

See practical livelihood options and nearby opportunities. Training, mentoring and certification build the market-ready capability you need to move forward with confidence.

03

Finance & Setup

Access funding, insurance and operational support at the right moment. Registration, documentation, packaging and logistics help remove the barriers that usually stall a good idea.

04

Link & Grow

Connect to buyers, markets and service networks that turn effort into income — with ongoing coordination that supports stronger reach, stability and growth over time.

Behind every step

Ecosystem partners, coordinated around you.

Government, banks, training institutes, logistics providers, insurers and buyers all work from the same shared view — so support doesn't stop once you move to the next stage.
