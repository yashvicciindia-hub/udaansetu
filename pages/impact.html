Roadmap

Scaling through partnerships — ecosystem by ecosystem, not city by city.

Build trust locally, prove value fast, then scale through aligned partners.

Year 1

Pilot

Launch the core platform in one pilot district and prove the end-to-end model.

  • Secure district-level alignment
  • Onboard the first users and enterprises
Year 2

State Expansion

Replicate the pilot across priority districts through state partners.

  • Standardise onboarding and support
  • Build a stronger regional pipeline
Year 3

Ecosystem Integration

Bring in CSR, enterprises, buyers and service providers.

  • Create recurring partnership agreements
  • Strengthen demand-side pull
Year 4–5

National Rollout

Scale nationally through mission-aligned institutions and networks.

  • Establish multi-state replication
  • Position the platform as core infrastructure
